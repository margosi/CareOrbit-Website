Brand guidelines
THE LOGO
The mark spirals a multicolor orbit of dots around a heart — care held at the center of a connected system.
Use the Trust-Blue heart lockup on light backgrounds and the all-white lockup on Ink Navy or photography.
Keep clear space of at least the heart's height on all sides; never recolor the orbit or stretch the mark.
