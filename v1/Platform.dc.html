The EHR workflow

In Epic, an orbit is ordered the same way as bloodwork.

Staff place an order for an orbit like any other order. The patient receives their journey by email; the care team sees activation, engagement, and assessment results come back on their dashboard. No new system to learn, no separate login for clinicians.

For teams outside that workflow, orbits issue from a provided patient list, a QR code, a text message, or on-site registration. A flat-file integration pattern is also available for auto-issuing orbits to pre-consented patients.
