ABOUT
Ten years building patient engagement that health focused organizations can actually run.
CareOrbit is a Digital Patient Engagement Platform designed to support any patient care journey through education, understanding, motivation, and insight-driven improvement. We are a Total Orbit company, built in St. Louis, shaped by more than a decade of work alongside Washington University, Siteman Cancer Center, and BJC HealthCare.
Leadership
Lean, senior, and hands-on with every client. The people you meet on the first call are the people who build your orbit.
Michael Margraf — CEO
Michael runs all facets of the business and stays personally involved in every client relationship. He has spent nearly 20 years leading technology startups, including as CEO of FireHydrant Mobile and co-founder of OSI. He holds a BBA from Washington University in St. Louis and serves on the board of CaringBoost.
Jeff Coburn — Co-founder &amp; Chief Strategy Officer
Jeff is the co-inventor of the orbit and heads the engagement side of the company: strategy, content, and how every orbit earns a patient's attention. His career spans creative strategy and engagement leadership for agencies and corporations, most recently as Principal at Ponder Concepts.
David Scott — Co-founder
David is a Fortune 500 executive and serial entrepreneur with multiple exits, a board member across several companies, and a former accelerator leader. He holds a J.D., cum laude, from St. Louis University School of Law and guides operations and commercial strategy.
