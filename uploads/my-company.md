## What an "orbit" is

An **orbit** is a deployable digital patient experience built around a specific clinical journey (a surgery type, a condition, a service line).

It includes patient education content, engagement touchpoints, and reporting. It's ordered by hospital staff the same way they'd order blood work inside Epic. The patient receives it on their device. The care team gets analytics on activation, usage, and outcomes.

Three structural variants of an orbit:

1. **Department-Level Orbit** — covers all patients in a department (e.g., all Oncology Surgery patients).
2. **Journey-Specific Orbit** — narrowed to a specific clinical journey (e.g., Pancreatic Cancer Surgery).
3. **Per-Practice / Per-Location customization** — light customization of an existing orbit for a specific clinic location.

Designed for **rapid build and scale.** Orbits can be built within a quarter using pre-trained pool of professionals; the system was deliberately stripped of approval bottlenecks and elite-experience dependencies so it's manageable by a wide range of staff.

### How we deliver: blueprints + three customization levels

We always begin from **proven use cases ("blueprints")** and then customize for the client. There are **three levels of customization**, and pricing depends on the level chosen:

1. **Low** — minimal changes to an existing blueprint.
2. **Medium** — moderate tailoring to the client's journey, content, and workflow.
3. **Full / Custom** — fully custom build for the client's specific needs.

On top of the build/customization, every client pays a **recurring monthly service fee** to use the orbit and for us to keep it updated, provide customer support, and deliver new releases.

> Note: this customization-level framing (Low / Medium / Full) is a different axis from the three *structural variants* above (Department-Level / Journey-Specific / Per-Location). Keep both; they describe different things — what an orbit covers vs. how much it's customized.

## The problem we solve

Hospitals deliver clinical care well, but their patient education and engagement layer is fragmented, inconsistent across service lines, and dependent on staff who are stretched thin. The result: avoidable readmissions, cancelled surgeries from unprepared patients, redundant office calls, lower HCAHPS scores, and patient compliance problems.

CareOrbit replaces that with a deployable, journey-specific digital experience that the care team can issue inside their existing Epic workflow.

## Pricing

Price is driven by the **level of customization** the client wants, starting from a proven blueprint:

- **Low / Medium / Full (Custom)** — the build/customization fee scales with the level chosen (see "How we deliver").
- **Monthly service fee (recurring)** — every client pays this to use the orbit and for us to keep it updated, provide customer support, and ship new releases.

## Product / tech stack (for due diligence and IT conversations)

- **Frontend:** React (Orbit Application, Dashboard, eScreening — repos in BitBucket cloud)
- **Backend:** Microservices
- **Database:** MySQL (Dev, Staging, Production)
- **Hosting:** AWS (HIPAA-compliant), deployed via Jenkins and Amplify
- **EHR integration:** Epic — orders for health education placed the same way as orders for blood work
- **Compliance:** HIPAA-compliant per BAA with AWS and Paubox (email)
- **PHI collected:** First name, last name, email, phone, diagnosis only. Stored HIPAA-compliantly. Never transmitted outside CareOrbit. Retained only for duration requested by customer; deleted on formal request.

## Healthcare enterprise reality (the things to never forget)

- Procurement is slow.
- Stakeholder fragmentation is real (clinical, IT, compliance, legal, ops all weigh in).
- Compliance sensitivity is high. HIPAA, consent, BAA all enter the conversation early.
- Integration fatigue. Hospitals have been burned by point solutions that don't play nicely.
- Operational burden concerns. They want low-friction implementations.
