## A typical week

Most of my time goes to sales: chasing new leads, preparing decks and proposals for my own leads and for my sales team. Beyond sales, in any given week I'm also:

- **Directing development** — Jim Peck heads the team. Final decisions on development efforts (anything not related to building orbits) fall on me. I'm often late reviewing items I need to comment on or approve.
- **Running client services** — most of it routes through me, including generating reports and holding quarterly client service meetings. Heather and Trish are POC for a couple of clients.
- **Handling legal** — negotiating contracts, sending and signing NDAs.
- **Owning capital raising and investor relations** — 100% of it, including the decks. I do it all for these efforts.
- **Approving financial decisions and reviews** — Melissa Margraf is our bookkeeper, but most financial calls land on my desk.

A great week is one where I have enough time and focus left over to actually generate 5 new leads for myself, make sales calls, and have a few strategic coffees with new prospects. A great week is also one where I can catch up on outstanding tasks like updating and sending out investor information.
