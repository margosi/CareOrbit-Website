A digital guide that keeps cardiac patients on plan, between every visit

An orbit is a digital support guide, sent by text or printed on a card. Inside: the medications, the daily weights, the warning signs, and what rehab asks of them. Programs run one per condition or procedure, and it reports back on who is keeping up.

Why it matters here

Orbits that keep cardiac patients and their families engaged and educated between visits.

Cardiac outcomes are decided at home: whether the medication is taken, whether sodium and weight are watched, whether rehab continues past the first hard week, whether a warning sign is recognized in time.

What a cardiology solution looks like

Any orbit solution can be versioned to fit.

Your cardiology solution is the set of orbits your service line actually needs: heart failure, AFib, post-PCI, cardiac rehab. Each is versioned by audience, depth, and stage. As a patient moves from diagnosis to procedure to rehab and long-term management, their orbit updates at the right moment, with no new link or login.

By depth
Success plan or full journey
A heart failure success plan carries broad, all-patient content and asks little of the care team. A full journey orbit layers condition-level specifics on top of that foundation.
Chosen per condition
By stage
Diagnosis through long-term management
Pre-procedure, discharge, rehab, and maintenance each get their own version, updating as the patient moves.

By procedure
Named orbits, plus a template
PCI, ablation, valve, and device implant can each carry their own orbit. Lower-volume procedures run on a template you version with minor edits.
