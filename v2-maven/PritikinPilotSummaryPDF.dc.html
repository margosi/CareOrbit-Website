Nine-month pilot with results
Pritikin Intensive Cardiac Rehabilitation · one outlet at a leading Midwest health system · pilot months 1–9, reported December 2023

Patients who opened their orbit started cardiac rehab at more than twice the rate.

Over nine months, 187 patients referred to Pritikin ICR were invited to a cardiac rehab orbit. Of those who activated it, 52% went on to start the program. Of those who never activated, 24% did.
