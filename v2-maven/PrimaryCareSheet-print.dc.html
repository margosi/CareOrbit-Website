Primary care orbit solutions

Your guidance in every patient's hands, all year between visits.

A year of health is managed in a few short visits; the rest happens at home, from memory. A primary care orbit adds a consumer-grade digital mentor alongside the after-visit summary and the follow-up call: engaging video, email, and text outreach in your brand and your clinicians' words, with engagement tracked at every step.

For the patient
A clear understanding of their conditions and plan. Your instructions and reminders on the day each one applies. Which symptoms mean call us, and which mean go to the ED.
For your team
Ordered from the workflow you already run, no new FTEs. Fewer calls asking what the visit already covered. Your brand and your protocols, not a generic app.
For the practice
Who opened their orbit and who never did, by patient. Check-ins routed back before the next visit. Optional validated measures, scored automatically.

Issued from the workflow you already run: the CareOrbit app, a scheduled flat file from any EHR, a patient list, a QR code, or a text message. No new app or login for the patient. No new FTEs. White-label, in your brand and your clinicians' voice. Live in a quarter, from kickoff to first patient.

For employers and plan sponsors
When a new plan or primary care benefit is introduced, an orbit adds to the enrollment packet and the benefits meeting: a guided introduction at open enrollment that improves employee acceptance of the change, onboarding for employees and families into the new benefit, and reinforcement through the year of covered preventive visits, screenings, and in-network care. Employer-branded versions can be issued by worksite or plan population, with engagement visible to the benefits team.
