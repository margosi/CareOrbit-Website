# Image prompts: second image on each orbit solutions page

The second image sits beside the "value" section, directly under the hero. At this point the prospect (service line director, clinical ops leader, practice administrator) has read the promise and is now reading *what an orbit actually is*: guidance the patient carries home, alongside the discharge folder and the follow-up call.

So the ideal image is **not** a product screenshot and **not** a stock hospital hallway. It is the human moment the orbit supports, with a phone or tablet present but secondary. It should make the buyer think "that is my patient, on the day this goes wrong."

## Shared rules for all nine

Apply to every prompt below.

- **Aspect ratio 4:5 portrait, 1080 x 1350 minimum.** The slot is a tall crop, roughly 560px wide by 420-600px tall, cropped to cover. Keep the subject centered vertically with headroom.
- Documentary photography, natural available light, shallow depth of field, warm neutral grade. Not glossy corporate stock, not clinical blue-grey.
- Real-looking people, unposed, mid-moment. No direct-to-camera smiling. No thumbs up.
- No visible PHI: no readable charts, screens, wristbands, badges, or whiteboards.
- No logos, no brand marks, no text overlays.
- A phone or tablet may appear but must be small in frame and screen-illegible or off-angle. The orbit is the guidance, not the device.
- Diverse, age-appropriate casting for the condition. Where the patient is older, cast older.
- Environments should read as real US health system spaces: exam rooms, infusion bays, home kitchens, clinic corridors. Slightly lived-in, not showroom.
- Avoid masks unless the setting genuinely requires one.

## 1. Oncology
**Slot id:** `oncology-value` · suggested filename `value-oncology.png`

> A woman in her 60s sitting in an infusion chair in a quiet outpatient oncology bay, a warm blanket over her lap, her adult daughter leaning in from the chair beside her, both looking at something on the daughter's phone held low between them. Late afternoon light through a window blind. Soft focus on an IV pole in the background. Calm, unhurried, private.

**Why this moment:** the oncology buyer's pain is the between-visit phone call and the panicked family member. Show the family member being included, because the orbit is what they read.

## 2. Cardiology
**Slot id:** `cardiology-value` · suggested filename `value-cardiology.png`

> A man in his late 60s at his own kitchen table in the morning, a weekly pill organizer and a blood pressure cuff in front of him, his phone face-down beside a mug. He is mid-thought, looking at the cuff readout, not at the camera. Home kitchen, ordinary and slightly cluttered, natural window light.

**Why this moment:** cardiology is managed at home for life. The whole argument of the page is that almost nothing happens in your building. Put the buyer in the patient's kitchen.

---

## 3. Orthopedics
**Slot id:** `ortho-value` · suggested filename `value-orthopedics.png`

> A physical therapist kneeling beside a woman in her 50s doing a seated knee extension in a bright outpatient rehab gym, the therapist's hand guiding her shin, both concentrating on the movement. Resistance band and a low step nearby. Mid-morning light, motion in the frame.

**Why this moment:** ortho's promise is patients who arrive ready and finish their home program. Show the effort and the coaching, not the surgery.

---

## 4. Women's Health
**Slot id:** `womenshealth-value` · suggested filename `value-womenshealth.png`

> A pregnant woman in her 30s sitting on the edge of an exam table, still in her own clothes, in conversation with a midwife or OB seated on a stool at her eye level, notes on a lap not a screen. Warm exam room, plant on the counter, soft daylight. The tone is a real conversation between equals.

**Why this moment:** the differentiator is confidence and preparation across a long pathway. Eye-level seating signals partnership, which is exactly what the maternity buyer is trying to sell to her own patients.

---

## 5. Bariatrics
**Slot id:** `bariatrics-value` · suggested filename `value-bariatrics.png`

> A woman in her 40s walking a neighborhood sidewalk in the early evening in ordinary athletic clothes, phone in an armband or in hand at her side, mid-stride, looking ahead. Autumn trees, low golden light, suburban street. Determined and everyday, not a fitness transformation shot.

**Why this moment:** bariatrics is a months-long qualification path that most candidates abandon. Show sustained, unglamorous progress, and cast respectfully. Avoid before/after framing entirely.

---

## 6. Behavioral Health
**Slot id:** `behavioralhealth-value` · suggested filename `value-behavioralhealth.png`

> A young adult sitting in a soft armchair in a warm therapy office, a therapist opposite in the same kind of chair, both mid-conversation, the client's posture open but tired. Lamp light rather than overhead fluorescents, textured rug, window with sheer curtain. Private and safe.

**Why this moment:** the page sells measurement-based care without administrative weight. The image should carry the therapeutic alliance so the screening data reads as care, not paperwork.

---

## 7. Medication Therapy & Adherence
**Slot id:** `medtherapy-value` · suggested filename `value-medtherapy.png`

> A pharmacist in a white coat standing at a private consultation counter, turning a medication box in her hands as she explains something to a man in his 50s across the counter. He is listening, slightly frowning, working it out. Retail or health-system pharmacy, shelving softly out of focus behind.

**Why this moment:** the buyer's problem is patients who start therapy and quietly stop when side effects hit. Show the counseling that normally happens once and then never again.

---

## 8. Surgical Support
**Slot id:** `surgical-value` · suggested filename `value-surgical.png`

> A pre-op nurse standing beside a gurney in a pre-operative bay, one hand resting on the rail, talking a man in his 60s through what happens next. He is in a gown, calm, listening. His wife is seated in the chair at the foot of the bed. Curtained bay, clean daylight, quiet before the procedure.

**Why this moment:** the surgical promise is patients who arrive prepared and do not call the office five times. Show the exact conversation that gets rushed on the day.

## 9. Behavioral Safety
**Slot id:** `safety-crew` · suggested filename `value-safety.png`

## Optional: a tenth, if you want a shared fallback

A generic value image for any future orbit page: a patient in their own living room in the evening, reading something on a phone held in both hands, a printed discharge folder open on the couch beside them. Makes the "adds to the folder, does not replace it" point in one frame.
