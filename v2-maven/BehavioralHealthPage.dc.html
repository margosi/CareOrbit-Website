The understanding gap

The six days between sessions.

A weekly session is one hour out of one hundred and sixty-eight. Recovery, relapse, medication decisions, and the choice to come back next week all happen in the other one hundred and sixty-seven, at home, where the program has almost no presence. That is the understanding gap, and the figures below are what it costs.
