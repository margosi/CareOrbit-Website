How a solution is built

Any protocol or cohort, multiple versions per orbit, fully customized.

Participant support comes first: consent, orientation, and the assessment schedule your protocol defines. Retention work then runs in the same place. You choose which orbits your study launches, and each is built to your protocol, your coordinators, and your population.
